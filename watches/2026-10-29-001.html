Top story

Watches of Switzerland expands its Rolex store in Glasgow

Watches of Switzerland, the UK's largest Rolex retailer, has unveiled an expanded Rolex store in Glasgow. It is another sign the group is putting money into UK showrooms rather than pulling back. More Rolex floor space in Scotland won't clear waiting lists for steel sports models overnight, but steadier new supply tends to soften pre-owned premiums on the more common references over time. WWD

New releases
Sinn 556 I FAZ II limited editionsc. £1,780
Part of Sinn's autumn novelties: green and orange limited editions of the 38.5 mm 556, on the Sellita SW200-1, at $2,370 in the US. The 556 is one of the easiest Sinns to sell second-hand in the UK. Loud limited dials appeal to collectors but can take longer to shift than the standard black. Worn & Wound
WM Brown x Timexc. £260
Matt Hranek's WM Brown and Timex have made a tank-style steel dress watch, sold as a set with a bracelet and a leather strap for $349. UK availability isn't confirmed yet. Timex collaborations usually sell out fast and turn up second-hand soon after, so they are worth watching as cheap, quick-turnover stock. Hodinkee

Industry and business
  • Baume & Mercier is now settling in under Italian jeweller Damiani, after Richemont completed the sale of the loss-making brand in July. A new owner often means a reset of ranges and pricing, so expect older Clifton and Riviera stock to be discounted through grey and pre-owned channels. The New York Times
  • Hodinkee's Business of Watches podcast reads New York Watch Week as proof that the US is still driving global demand for premium and luxury watches. Strong American buying pulls good pre-owned stock towards the US, so UK dealers may find more competition when sourcing. Hodinkee
