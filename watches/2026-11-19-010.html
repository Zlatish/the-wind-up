Top story

Wristcheck wants a global standard for pre-owned watches

Hong Kong platform Wristcheck is launching the Global Timepiece Standard (GTS), opening up its in-house 10-step authentication and grading process to the wider trade. Certified watches come with an inspection report, timing results in five positions and tamper-evident sealed packaging; Wristcheck says it has graded more than 10,000 watches across 300+ brands, worth over US$200 million. No partners, prices or UK availability have been named yet, so it only matters if other dealers adopt it, but a shared grading language would be useful for any UK pre-owned business pricing condition consistently. New releases
Frederique Constant Classic Tourbillon Perpetual Calendar Manufacture 40 mmc. £21,300 (€26,995)
Ref. FC-975N3H6 shrinks FC's tourbillon perpetual calendar into a 40 mm, 12.69 mm steel case with a sapphire marquetry dial, powered by the in-house FC-975 automatic (38 hours). It is limited to 88 numbered pieces. FC has long been the cheapest route to this pairing of complications, and earlier FC tourbillons have tended to sell well under list second-hand in the UK, so watch for a discount curve here too. Monochrome
Union Glashütte Averin Date (square, integrated bracelet)c. £1,950 (€2,450)
Union Glashütte's Averin goes square and integrated: 37 x 37 mm steel, 9.85 mm thick, 100 m water resistance and the ETA 2892-based UNG-07.S1 with a silicon hairspring and at least 60 hours of reserve. It comes in midnight blue, silver and aquamarine. It is a German-made integrated sports watch for under £2,000, a price point where UK buyers have few alternatives beyond Tissot's PRX. Monochrome

Did you know?

Watch adverts used to show 8:20, not 10:10

Watch adverts from the 1920s and 1930s mostly set the hands at 8:20; 10:10 took over from the 1950s. Both are symmetrical and keep the logo clear, but a 2017 study in Frontiers in Psychology found that 10:10 reads as a smiling face and 8:20 as a sad one. Watches shown at 10:10 drew higher pleasure ratings than those at 8:20 or 11:30. Frontiers in Psychology
