Top story

Watches of Switzerland lifts its outlook after a £974m first half

The UK's biggest Rolex retailer grew currency-adjusted revenue 15% to £974m in the six months to 1 November, with the US up 23% to £501m and the UK up 9% to £473m. It has narrowed full-year growth guidance to 7 to 10%, and adjusted profit is expected to rise about 28%. For UK pre-owned buyers, steady new-watch demand on the high street keeps waiting lists alive and supports secondary prices for the big Rolex references; full results follow on 10 December. interactive investor

Two fully brushed steel divers rated to 500 m: a 44 mm left-handed Destro on the P.980 and a 42 mm GMT on the P.900, both with 72-hour reserves. Did you know?

The most complicated watch was made without a computer

Patek Philippe's Henry Graves Supercomplication, delivered in 1933, packs 24 functions, including Westminster chimes and a star chart of the night sky over Graves's Fifth Avenue apartment. It held the title of most complicated watch for more than 50 years until Patek's own Calibre 89 in 1989, and it is still the most complicated made without computer help. In 2014 it sold at Sotheby's Geneva for CHF 23.2 million. Wikipedia
