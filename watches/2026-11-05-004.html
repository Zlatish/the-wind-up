Top story

Omega quietly discontinues the Speedmaster X-33

Fratello reports that the Speedmaster X-33 Marstimer, developed with ESA engineers for Mars missions, has been removed from Omega's catalogue, ending a line that ran from 1998 to 2026. Omega has not said whether a successor is coming. For a UK pre-owned buyer, discontinued references with a cult following tend to firm up once new stock dries up, so clean, boxed X-33s are worth watching now rather than later. Fratello

Did you know?

Why Snoopy rides on some Speedmasters

Snoopy is NASA's safety mascot, and the Silver Snoopy is the award astronauts give for work that keeps crews safe. When Apollo 13 was crippled in 1970, Jack Swigert's Speedmaster timed the 14-second engine burn that set the crew on course for home. Omega received a Snoopy award for its part, and has marked it since with Snoopy editions, now some of the most sought-after Speedmasters. Wikipedia
