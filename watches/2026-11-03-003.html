Top story

Audemars Piguet unveils three Supersonnerie repeaters and two stone-dial Code 11.59s

AP has added five references: Royal Oak Minute Repeater Supersonneries in titanium (50 pieces, fumé green dial) and pink gold, a white gold Code 11.59 Supersonnerie with a smoked sapphire dial, and two 38 mm Code 11.59s with gold obsidian and onyx dials at US$57,500 and US$68,500 before tax (c. £43,000 and c. £51,000). The repeaters are price on request. For a UK pre-owned buyer these sit well above most dealers' stock, but the 38 mm Code 11.59 shows AP still pushing the smaller, dressier size, which is worth watching as earlier Code 11.59s reach the secondary market. New releases
H. Moser & Cie. Heritage Centre Seconds and Heritage Chronographc. £14,800 to c. £27,400
Moser's vintage-styled Heritage line returns in a 42 mm steel drum case: a time-only Espresso fumé (200 pieces, CHF 15,700) and a White fumé chronograph (100 pieces, CHF 29,200). Small runs from a brand with a loyal following tend to hold value better than Moser's core models, so they are worth noting for later resale. Monochrome
Casio CMK-100c. £260 to c. £350
Casio's second mechanical range is a 38 mm titanium, Explorer-style watch with 100 m water resistance and a Japanese automatic movement, at EUR 299 on a strap or EUR 399 on a bracelet. At this price it will be a gift-season seller new rather than a pre-owned stock line, but it puts pressure on entry-level Seiko and Orient. Monochrome

Industry and business
  • Robb Report looks at why Rolex, Audemars Piguet and Patek Philippe dominate a secondary watch market it puts at US$26.6 billion (c. £20bn). For a new UK dealer, it is a reminder that the big three set liquidity: they are the easiest stock to sell on, but also the most competitive to buy. Robb Report
  • The Geneva autumn sales are lining up, with Hodinkee counting more than 1,225 watches across four auction houses. Phillips is also offering a rare Cartier Portico clock estimated at up to US$3.9m (c. £3m). Results from these sales will be the best read on high-end prices going into 2027. Hodinkee
