Story of the week

Audemars Piguet launches The Watch Keepers, its own certified pre-owned platform

AP now authenticates, grades and resells its own used watches online, starting in Switzerland and Europe (so the UK is in scope from day one). Sellers set a fixed price or run a bidding format, the watch is held by AP until it sells, and buyers get a certificate and a two-year warranty. After Rolex CPO hit a record $229m in the third quarter, expect brand-certified stock to set the ceiling on pre-owned Royal Oak prices, and expect independent dealers to compete on price, speed and service rather than on trust. Hodinkee

New releases
Nomos Club 38c. £1,310
The Club grows from 36 mm to 38 mm and keeps its hand-wound in-house Alpha calibre. It's one of the best-value entries into German watchmaking, and it's a permanent collection model, so supply won't be scarce. Monochrome
Breitling Navitimer B09 Chronograph 41c. £6,000
Breitling's core Navitimer finally gets a hand-wound chronograph, alongside a Cosmonaute at about $10,600. It's aimed squarely at enthusiasts, and it may soften demand for older manual-wind Navitimers on the secondary market. Worn & Wound
Sinn 140 A S Anniversaryc. £4,700
A 65th-anniversary spaceflight chronograph, limited to 300 and sold with both bracelet and strap. Sinn limited editions tend to hold their value well in the UK and German markets. Fratello
Grand Seiko SBGY045c. £8,200
A 38 mm ultra-thin manual-wind Spring Drive with a fresh take on the Omiwatari dial. It's limited to 600 and sold only through the GS9 Club application process, which usually means a premium on resale. Fratello

Dealer's notebook

Brand CPO is now the reference price for Rolex and AP, so stock those models only when you can sit clearly under the official certified price. Record listings and slower sell-through mean there is no need to chase. Wait for motivated sellers. Value pieces like the PRX, Nomos and Sinn turn over quickly and tie up little cash while you learn the market.
