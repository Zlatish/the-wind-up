Story of the week

Christie's makes CHF 47.8m in its best-ever watch sale

Christie's Rare Watches sale in Geneva on 9 November made CHF 47.8m ($58.7m, c. £44m), a record for a watch sale at the house, with all 218 lots sold. The top lot was F.P. Journe's pink gold Vagabondage III at CHF 2.5m (c. £2.3m), more than three times its high estimate, and the Vagabondage I and II added CHF 2m and CHF 2.2m. Coming days after Phillips' CHF 92m sale, it confirms the top of the market is in rude health, but the money is concentrated in independents and rarities rather than the everyday steel that a UK pre-owned dealer trades. New releases
Brellum Duobox 39 TC Moonphase Chronometerc. £3,300 before VAT
A blue-dial, 46-piece limited edition with a triple calendar, moonphase and COSC-certified Valjoux 7751-based chronograph in a 39 mm case. It costs CHF 3,600 before VAT and is sold only through Brellum's own site. Proper complications at this price are rare, but small Swiss brands like this are slow to resell in the UK. Monochrome
Zenith G.F.J. Calibre 135 Double Signed J.N. Shapiroc. £63,600
Zenith's second Double Signed collaboration pairs American watchmaker Josh Shapiro's hand guilloché dial with damaskeened bridges on the hand-wound Calibre 135. It is 39 mm in rose gold, limited to 25 pieces at CHF 68,900, through Zenith retailers. Strictly a collector piece, landing in the same month Zenith opened talks on cutting jobs in Le Locle. Time+Tide
Wren Diver One Titanium Glacierc. £1,120 (US price)
The New York brand's Swiss-made diver returns in DLC-coated grade 5 titanium with an ice-blue gradient dial, ETA 2824 movement and 200 m water resistance. The first run is 100 numbered pieces at $1,495. Interesting spec for the money, but with no UK retail presence it is one to watch rather than stock. Time+Tide

Did you know?

Citizen was named by the Mayor of Tokyo

Citizen's first pocket watch, made in 1924 by the Shokosha Watch Research Institute, had no name. Tokyo's mayor, Count Shinpei Goto, was asked to suggest one and chose "CITIZEN", hoping ordinary people would treasure the watch for years. When the Citizen Watch Company was founded in 1930, it simply took the name of that first watch. Fitting, perhaps, for a brand now celebrating 50 years of its light-powered Eco-Drive. Citizen

Dealer's notebook

Record Geneva sales are good for confidence but say little about the £1,000 to £10,000 stock a new UK dealer will hold. Richemont's growth came mostly from Cartier jewellery, while its watch brands grew a slower 10%, so retail demand for watches is steady rather than booming. The live opportunity is in well-reviewed British and independent pieces under £1,000, such as Studio Underd0g, which sell quickly second-hand. Buy a few, watch how fast they move, and keep the bulk of your money in proven names.

Also this week
  • Industry. Richemont's sales rose 17% at constant rates to €12.3bn in the six months to 30 September, with operating profit up 33%. Its Specialist Watchmakers grew 10%, led by Vacheron Constantin, Jaeger-LeCoultre and A. Lange & Söhne, while Europe was up 13%. Monochrome
  • Industry. The Patek Philippe Museum in Geneva marks 25 years since Philippe Stern opened it in November 2001. It holds one of the world's most important horological collections and is worth a visit for anyone learning the history behind the brands they will trade. Monochrome
  • Industry. Vacheron Constantin held the first Concours d'Élégance Horlogère, a judged show of historic watches modelled on classic car concours. It is another sign of brands using heritage events to build collector loyalty. Hodinkee
  • Trend watch. Independents keep pulling the money: F.P. Journe's Vagabondage trio made over CHF 6.7m at Christie's, and Singapore's IAMWATCH show is filling feeds with small artisanal makers. Expect more collector interest, and higher prices, for well-known independents' entry pieces. Revolution
  • Trend watch. Exotic metals are spreading to affordable watches: Wren's coated titanium diver and Vario's tantalum Futurist both landed this week. Titanium is now an expected option under £1,500, which is worth remembering when pricing older steel pieces. Time+Tide
  • Coming up. Studio Underd0g's 04Series goes on sale on 17 November at underd0g.com and by appointment at its Maidenhead showroom, at £750. Monochrome
  • Coming up. "Vintage Watches: The Modern Guide" by Eric Wind and Charlie Dunne goes on sale on 17 November, aimed at newcomers and long-time collectors alike: useful reading before buying vintage stock. Hodinkee
  • Coming up. Phillips previews its New York Watch Auction XV in Hong Kong from 18 to 22 November, ahead of the December sale. Phillips
